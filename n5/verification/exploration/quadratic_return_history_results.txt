Parameterized rational-return history certificate (2026-09-04)
================================================================

Scope
-----
The calculation retains the first high product.  It allows an arbitrary
affine correction, every first-order target correction, and the returned
quadratic section itself.  It then multiplies by an arbitrary affine factor
whose quadratic part is one of r0, r1, or rInfinity.  The return and feedback
product are required to be quadratic, and their difference is required to
be the missing first-order target class.  Unpopulatedness and nonzero-high
are not used by the contradiction.

Exact algebraic ledger
----------------------
There are four simultaneous factor-pair types:

  zero_one, one_one, one_two, one_three.

For one_two and one_three, all three rational feedback directions have a
degree-one polynomial certificate (64--122 generator multiples).

For zero_one and one_one, rational-place symmetry leaves an aligned and an
off-axis direction.  The off-axis residual has eight bilinear terms.  Of its
96 residual-one Boolean branches, all 96 have degree-one certificates.

For the aligned direction the residual is

  e1*y6 + e2*y6 + e4*y6 + e4*y7
    + e6*y1 + e6*y2 + e6*y4 + e7*y4,

where e=ell in the zero_one case and e=mDifference in the one_one case.
Of its 480 residual-one branches, 474 have degree-one certificates.  The six
remaining branches are exactly GL(2,F2) on the (1,6) coordinates.  They are
one symmetry orbit.  Splitting the single returned-section correction bit
leaves two ideals.  Singular `liftstd` gives the unit ideal in both cases.

The zero_one certificates use 16 semantic generators; the one_one
difference-coordinate certificates use 13.  The multipliers are stored in
`quadratic_return_history_polynomial.py`.  The command

  python n5/verification/exploration/quadratic_return_history_polynomial.py \
    --case zero_one --direction r0 --verify-exceptional-certificate

independently reconstructs all four lift identities in a second sparse
Boolean-polynomial engine.  Every report ends with

  combination_is_one_in_boolean_quotient: True
  combination_remainder: 1

SAT cross-check
---------------
The direct ANF SAT encoding returned UNSAT for ten of the twelve named
factor-pair/direction cases without the unpopulatedness restriction.  The
two 300/600-second timeouts were zero_one/r1 and one_one/r1 in the original
coordinate ordering; in each case the fixed factor pair has a stabilizer
interchanging r1 and rInfinity, and the rInfinity branch returned UNSAT.
The polynomial calculation above also closes the off-axis orbit directly.

For the displayed kernel counterexample, the return-only positive control is
SAT and independently replays with minimum target-coset alternating rank 4.
Adding rational feedback makes both correction-bit branches UNSAT in 2.35 s
and 4.10 s on the local CryptoMiniSat run.

Trust boundary
--------------
This file records exact theorem-discovery certificates, not the final Lean
theorem.  Singular was used only to discover sparse multipliers.  The stored
multipliers are replayed by independent Python polynomial arithmetic.  The
remaining trusted step is to express these identities in Lean and connect
the factor-pair and rational-place normalizations to the circuit suffix.
